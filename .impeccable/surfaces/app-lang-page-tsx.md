# Surface: Kainos Dagang corporate site (home, catalogue, product pages)

Scope: `app/[lang]/page.tsx` (home), `app/[lang]/products` (catalogue), `app/[lang]/products/[slug]` (product detail). Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience: B2B buyers (contractors, factories, site supervisors) and individual workers in the Klang Valley. Job: find the right safety gear and ask about it. Action: WhatsApp a specific product (pre-filled with name, code, size, qty and link) or visit one of three branches. Proof: real signboard identity, three real branches with addresses, company registration, brands stocked (Black Hammer, Kickers). Constraints: no prices, no "authorised dealer" claim, sample products until the real list arrives, EN/BM/ZH.

Memorable moment: the site opens as the shop's own black signboard, and footsteps lead the visitor in.

Unresolved: real products and photos, opening hours, social links.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is the shop people have driven past. It opens on the black KEDAI KASUT signboard and "MASUK" walks the visitor in to the stock. It refuses the category default: a stock photo of a smiling worker in a hard hat, "Your Trusted Safety Partner", and three icon cards.

OWN-WORLD: Flat black acrylic sign faces (#000) with white Moderniz caps and the orange #FE5200 shield. The orange tagline band with black 13° hazard stripes stitches every section seam. The white shop floor carries faint grey bootprints. Orange owns whole fields (the band, the MASUK plaque, the directory hover) and is never used as sprinkled accents. Poppins for reading. The real sign furniture is functional: the legal "Dimiliki oleh" line, the category board, the aisle directory.

STORY: Recognise the shop → know in one line what it sells and where → MASUK into the shelves → WhatsApp the exact product, or walk into the nearest branch.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A full-bleed signboard, about 60% of the height. The nav runs along its top edge. The shield + KAINOS DAGANG wordmark sits at signage scale, with a one-line hook beside it. A category board strip is set into the sign as links, and the legal line runs along the bottom edge. Below it, the full-width tagline band. Then the shop floor: an orange MASUK door plaque (the primary action, into the catalogue) beside the first featured products, each with a WhatsApp button. The WhatsApp float is always bottom-right.

FORM: Signboard facade, #1 on my ordered list of 7 (dealt index 1, chosen by the user); seed key da51cc5d. Signature motion: "setiap langkah", bootprint steps that appear down the page as the visitor scrolls, scroll-driven with a static fallback.
